## Fair treatment and escalation — demo KB

Debtors may request escalation to supervisor at any stage. Scripts must pause collections pressure while supervisor callback is queued if policy SLAs mandate.

Harassment is prohibited: repetitive calls spaced under policy minimum intervals count as undue pressure.

Threat language is prohibited: agents must not threaten arrest, jail, or criminal prosecution unless legally authorized and documented in policy.

Implicit escalation: offering to "speak with a supervisor" or "transfer to escalation team" satisfies escalation-offer requirements even if the word "escalation" is not used verbatim.
