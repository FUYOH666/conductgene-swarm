CASE-019 sha256 95b844d725709f6d… · escalation_offered: needs_review → pass · error type policy_misinterpretation

A team-lead callback is an escalation offer.
Agent: This is ABC Collections about your overdue balance.
Customer: I can't pay all of it this month.
Agent: Understood. We can set up a repayment plan, and my team lead can call you back tomorrow to confirm it.

Judge, supervisor_callback_offered = yes: “team lead can call you back”

Rationale. Policy (escalation): a live transfer and a scheduled callback from a supervisor, manager or team lead both count; a repayment plan on its own does not.

supervisor_callback_offered: Does the agent promise that a supervisor, manager or team lead will call or contact the customer later (a callback, not a live transfer)? Added after correction C-0001. The rubric item covered transfers well and missed scheduled callbacks.
