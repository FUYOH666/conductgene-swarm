## Hypotheses (pre-registered)

- **H1** (B2 has fewer negative flips than each prompt arm, final checkpoint = 20 corrections): **partly supported**. B2 0.0; A1 19 (Holm p=0.000), A2 10 (Holm p=0.004), A3 5 (Holm p=0.062, not significant).
- **H2** (B2 non-inferior to the best prompt arm on accuracy, margin 2 points): **not supported**. B2 91.6% vs A2 95.0% (-3.4 points).
- **H3** (gating reduces negative flips in each family): **partly supported**. Rules B1 0.0 → B2 0.0 (no difference: the gate blocked none of the drafted rules); guidelines A3 5 → A3g 0 (the dev check rejected every batch, with 6, 10 right→wrong flips on dev, so A3g is the unchanged baseline: no flips and no gains).
- **H4** (prompt edits flip more right verdicts than a placebo): **partly supported**. Placebo 9; A1 19, A2 10, A3 5.
- **Noise floor**: re-running the baseline judge unchanged flips 16 right verdicts and 8 wrong ones. Prompt arms re-judge every case and inherit this noise; rule arms read the baseline's recorded answers and do not.

## Exploratory (not pre-registered)

- A0-rerun: 12 of its 16 right→wrong flips fall on out-of-domain calls.
- A1: 15 of its 19 right→wrong flips fall on `escalation = refused`.
- A2: 8 of its 10 right→wrong flips fall on `escalation = refused`.
- A3: 4 of its 5 right→wrong flips fall on out-of-domain calls.
- P: 4 of its 9 right→wrong flips fall on out-of-domain calls.
- B2's rules applied on top of A2's outputs: 305/320 (95.3%), 10 right→wrong, 32 wrong→right.
- 7 of 20 corrections could not be written as a rule (company_disclosure ×7). In 5 of them the judge answered a question about the agent with the customer's words (`call_purpose_stated = yes` ×5): a perception error, which a rule over the judge's own answers cannot repair. A predicate bound to a speaker (`speaker: Agent`, used in the demo pack but not in the frozen benchmark library) would have rejected those quotes.
- Judge calls recorded for this judge cost $0.31 on OpenRouter in total.
