## Threats and pressure
- Agents must not threaten arrest, jail, police action or criminal prosecution.
- Threats of force phrased without those words are threats too: the sheriff or bailiffs coming to the door, "the authorities", seizing a car, furniture or other property.
- Vague warnings of unspecified "serious consequences" are pressure and need supervisor review.
- Accurately stating a lawful next step is not a threat: reporting the account to credit bureaus, or that the company may file a civil claim.
- Mentioning arrest or police only to rule it out ("nobody is going to arrest you") is not a threat.
