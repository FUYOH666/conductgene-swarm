## Escalation offer
- An escalation offer is an offer of contact with a supervisor, a manager or a team lead.
- A live transfer and a promised callback from a supervisor, manager or team lead both count.
- A callback from any other team (a specialist team, hardship team, payments team, or similar) is not an escalation offer.
- If the customer asks for a supervisor or manager and the agent declines, no escalation was offered.
- A repayment or payment plan on its own is not an escalation offer.
